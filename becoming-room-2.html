07

Nexus

Nexus Nexus explores the space where separate histories, identities and experiences meet. The work considers identity not as something formed in isolation, but as a constantly shifting network of relationships between inheritance, memory, culture and lived experience. At its centre is the idea of connection—the invisible threads linking who we were, who we are becoming, and what we carry forward. Nexus reflects on the moments where past and present intersect, suggesting that transformation does not require abandoning what came before, but finding new meaning within it.

08

Cosmic Anchor

Cosmic Anchor explores the connection between the individual, ancestry, spirituality and destiny. Drawing on Igbo cosmological concepts including Ala, Chi, Ikenga, Mmụọ, Ibà and Oge, the work presents the human figure as a point where the visible and spiritual worlds converge, suggesting that identity is shaped by ancestral memory, cultural inheritance, place and the forces that guide one’s journey forward.

Anastasia

Anastasia reflects renewal after adversity. It considers the remarkable human ability to rebuild identity following loss, uncertainty, and change, suggesting that every ending quietly prepares the ground for another beginning.
