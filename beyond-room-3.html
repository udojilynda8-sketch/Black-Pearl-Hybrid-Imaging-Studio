Imagination reshapes reality. These works explore the relationship between identity, architecture, creativity and the worlds we construct within and around ourselves.

09

Metropolitan Muse

Metropolitan Muse meditates on the dialogue between humanity and the modern city. Towers of steel and glass become more than architecture—they become symbols of ambition, memory, isolation, and possibility. The work invites viewers to ask whether we shape our cities, or whether our cities quietly reshape us.

Obsidian Vision

Obsidian Vision questions the relationship between clarity and mystery. Like polished volcanic glass, the work appears to reveal while simultaneously concealing. It invites contemplation of the truths we pursue and the shadows we often overlook, suggesting that insight is sometimes found by embracing uncertainty rather than resisting it.

12

Mosaics

Mosaics envisions identity as a composition of countless experiences rather than a single fixed story. Each fragment—whether joyful, painful, ordinary, or extraordinary—contributes to a larger whole. The work reminds us that beauty often emerges not despite our broken pieces, but because of the way they come together.

13

Echoes of the Highrise

Echoes of the Highrise concludes Beyond by reflecting on the relationship between human ambition and inner silence. Towering structures become monuments to aspiration, yet behind every illuminated window lies an unseen story. The work invites contemplation of what remains when the noise of achievement fades, suggesting that the greatest journeys often unfold within rather than above us.
