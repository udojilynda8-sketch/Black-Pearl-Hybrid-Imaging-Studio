COLLECTION IV

Beyond

What lies beyond certainty?

Beyond explores perception, consciousness, balance, and the unseen dimensions of human experience. The collection invites viewers to look beyond what is visible and consider realities that exist between certainty and possibility.

Beyond

Beyond explores the unseen dimensions of human experience. Through themes of balance, perception, duality, and consciousness, the collection invites viewers to question certainty and embrace mystery. Rather than presenting definitive answers, each work offers a space for contemplation, encouraging personal interpretation and reminding us that reality is often richer than what can be immediately seen.

Equilibrium

Equilibrium explores the delicate balance between opposing forces that quietly shape existence. Light and shadow, certainty and doubt, movement and stillness coexist rather than compete, suggesting that harmony is not the absence of tension but the ability to hold it with grace.
