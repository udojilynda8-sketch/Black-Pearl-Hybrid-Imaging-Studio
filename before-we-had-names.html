COLLECTION I

Before We Had Names

Where do memories begin?

Memory begins long before our first conscious recollection. It is shaped by inherited stories, relationships, places and histories that quietly influence who we become.

COLLECTION STATEMENT

An Exploration of Memory

Before We Had Names examines the invisible foundations of identity. It reflects on the inherited stories, places, histories and quiet legacies that shape human lives before conscious memory begins. Rather than documenting the past, the collection asks how memory continues to influence who we become and who we choose to be.

01

Horizon Heir

Horizon Heir **Horizon Heir** explores the inheritance of identity beyond bloodlines, tracing the invisible bonds between ancestry, memory, and destiny. The work reflects on how the stories, sacrifices, and resilience of those who came before continue to shape the paths we walk today. Standing at the meeting point of history and possibility, it invites viewers to consider what they have inherited, what they choose to carry forward, and how the horizon of the future is shaped by the echoes of the past.
