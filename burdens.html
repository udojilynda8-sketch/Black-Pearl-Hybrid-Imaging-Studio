COLLECTION III

Burdens

What do we carry?

Some burdens are visible. Most are not. This exhibition explores the emotional weight carried quietly through every human life.

COLLECTION STATEMENT

The Weight We Cannot See

Burdens explores the invisible weight carried through grief, responsibility, memory, endurance and hope. Rather than portraying suffering alone, the exhibition reflects on humanity's remarkable capacity to continue moving forward despite what it carries. Each artwork invites viewers to recognise both vulnerability and strength within themselves and others.

01

Anatomy of Mourning

Anatomy of Mourning examines grief as a landscape rather than a moment. It reflects the unseen emotional terrain navigated after loss, acknowledging that mourning changes shape over time but never completely disappears. The work honours both sorrow and the quiet resilience that allows healing to begin.
